## Always

- Answer first. The ask or the answer is in the first sentence.
- Contractions are fine. Plain words over formal ones.
- No long dashes. Commas, colons or parentheses instead.
- No sign-off on short messages.

## Teammate ask in chat

- **What I asked for** (2026-11-20): "Too long. Lead with the ask, drop the background."
- **What I actually sent:** "Can you send the signed contract by Thursday? I need it for the renewal."
- **How to apply:** the ask is the first sentence, one reason at most, no recap of how we got here.

## Status update to the team

- **What I asked for** (2026-11-20): "Nobody reads five paragraphs. One line per thing."
- **What I actually sent:** "Renewal: signed, filed. Supplier switch: waiting on their quote, chasing Monday. Nothing needed from anyone."
- **How to apply:** one line per item, the state first, what happens next last. Say plainly when nothing is needed.

## Outside vendor, first contact

- **What I asked for** (2026-11-20): "Warmer, and tell them who we are before the ask."
- **What I actually sent:** "Hi Sam, I run operations at a small company and found you through your listing. Could you quote a monthly service for two sites? Happy to share details on a quick call."
- **How to apply:** one line on who you are and how you found them, then the ask, then an easy next step.
